    Where this came from

    A smaller cut of something I'd already built.

    Review Radar is a spin-off of Undersigned, a review ledger I built end to end in about five weeks as a publisher on the Solana dApp Store. I built it because I hit the problem Tiny's teams have: a star rating tells a developer nothing they can act on.

    When Andrew's post landed, I pointed the same idea at Tiny's public reviews. The pieces that make this page work came straight from Undersigned, including the look of this page.

    Watch the 3-minute demoOpen UndersignedSource
    • Reviews turned into problems a team can fixUndersigned watches 1,849 store apps every day; Review Radar runs the same reading over 8 Tiny apps.
    • Local models, every number checkedThe model drafts the words; the code computes every count, and a draft with a number it can't back is thrown out.
    • Agents as first-class usersUndersigned ships a 26-tool MCP server; Review Radar has its own, with 8 tools.
    • Every languageUndersigned’s app is in 28 languages; Review Radar translates Letterboxd’s reviews from 12 more countries, with the original a tap away.
